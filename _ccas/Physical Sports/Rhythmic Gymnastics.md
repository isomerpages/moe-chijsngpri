<h3><strong>Introduction</strong>&nbsp;</h3>
<p>Rhythmic Gymnastics&nbsp;is a sport that combines elements of ballet,
gymnastics, dance, and apparatus manipulation. It develops graceful movement,
flexibility, coordination, and dance moves combined with hand apparatus.
&nbsp;Hand apparatus include&nbsp;rope, hoop, ball, clubs, and ribbon.
Rhythmic Gymnasts can be performed individually or in a group.</p>
<h3><strong>Aims and Objectives</strong>&nbsp;</h3>
<p>Rhythmic Gymnastics as a CCA&nbsp;when started young, aims to prevent
undesirable developments and habits related to the body structure, at the
same time, enhances the cognitive aspects as well as the physical/coordination
skills of the gymnast. As a gymnast grows, she will continue to develop,
extend, and refine agility, balance, coordination and flexibility which
are required for higher level skills. Progressively, she will learn and
develop the fundamental and higher-level gymnastics skills needed for competition.
Also, she will be required to handle hand apparatus, which ensures progressive
participation in competitive gymnastics.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Gymnastics is a sport of learning a skill and then practising it to perfection.
With intentional and dedicated practice of the sport, the values of self-discipline
and perseverance are developed.</p>
<p></p>
<h3><strong>Character Development</strong>&nbsp;</h3>
<p>We hope to develop the following values through gymnastics:
<br>&nbsp;🙘 Respect
<br>&nbsp;🙘 Self-discipline
<br>&nbsp;🙘 Perseverance
<br>&nbsp;🙘 Patience
<br>&nbsp;🙘 Self-confidence
<br>&nbsp;🙘 Time-management</p>
<p>Gymnastics provides opportunities to develop skills in communication and
team building. In a team, pupils learn to treat others with respect. These
skills enable a pupil to function effectively as leaders, as well. Such
leadership skills are further honed when they carry out pupil-initiated
projects/events or lead during CCA Camp and Leadership Camp.</p>
<h3><strong>Programmes/Activities</strong>&nbsp;</h3>
<p>1. Selection and trial training module at Primary One</p>
<p>● How Do I Qualify for the CCA Programme?</p>
<p>a. Pupils with potential from Primary One will be identified during Physical
Education lessons while pupils from other levels (P2-P4) have to attend
a selection test.</p>
<p>b. Upon selection, the pupil has to attend a 2 phase trial training, which
is meant to be the next level of selection with higher levels of gymnastics
training components included.</p>
<p>c. Upon confirmation, the pupil will officially join the gymnastics team
as a CCA and has to be committed to the sports.</p>
<p>Note: Short-listed pupils will receive PG notification issued by the school.</p>
<p>● ^ Criteria for Attending Selection Test from P2 - P4</p>
<p>RGym is a competitive sport in our school. We only accept a gymnast at
higher levels if she has met the following minimum standards:</p>
<p>❖ P3 - Must have passed Level 2 or equivalent</p>
<p>❖ P4 - Must have passed Level 3 or equivalent</p>
<p>For a competitive gymnast, the recommended age to achieve level 2 competency
is 8 years old under national school competition ruling. All gymnasts need
to master and pass level 2 skills before they can move on to train and
participate in competitions at higher levels.</p>
<p>2. Competitions and 1 Performance in a year</p>
<p>● National Primary Schools Rhythmic Gymnastics Championships</p>
<p>● In-House Rhythmic Gymnastics Competition (alternate years)</p>
<p>● National Day Performance or Teachers’ Day Performance</p>
<p>** Participants for competitions and performances are selected by Teacher
in-charge and Coach through a series of Selection Trials conducted during
CCA sessions.</p>
<h3><strong>Benefits</strong>&nbsp;</h3>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Reduced risks of injuries as exercises are varied and can be adapted to
fit the age&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The sport does not depend upon weather, hence can be practised both indoors
and outdoors (with proper mat in use)&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Develops the conditional and coordination skills, and to improve the flexibility
of the joints&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cognitive and psycho/socio development&nbsp;</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Contributes to the expansion of an individual’s basic sport culture</p>
</li>
</ul>
