<h5><strong>Aims &amp; Objectives</strong></h5>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>To instil resilience and confidence in our players</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To promote sportsmanship and teamwork</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>To groom potential and promising young players</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h5><strong>Character Development&nbsp;</strong></h5>
<p>●&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Pupils&nbsp;will learn to develop&nbsp;self-discipline,
resilience and commitment through their trainings and competitions. Through
these interactions, our pupils will be able to develop in their social-emotional
competences as they learn to respect their peers and competitors.</p>
<p>●&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Through the practices and matches, our pupils
will:</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;build confidence</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;develop problem solving and
communication skills</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;develop cooperative skills
to promote harmony and inclusiveness in the group</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;develop good sportsmanship</p>
<h5><strong>Programmes /Activities</strong></h5>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Warming-up exercises: such as footwork, stretching and running.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Drills and skill practices to improve the players’ strength and abilities.
<br>e.g. Backhand, forehand, pair-practice shots etc.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Sparring matches during trainings to increase the players’ cooperation,
competiveness and readiness.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Friendly matches, cooling down exercises and games help to promote the
love for the sport and camaraderie among our players.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h5><strong>Benefits</strong></h5>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Train pupils to be resilient</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Foster team spirit</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Improve health and fitness</p>
</li>
</ul>
